# Community Code of Conduct

This project welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels and
hardware setups. Help keep issues, discussions, reviews and other project spaces
respectful and useful.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate, patient and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different experiences and technical knowledge. Ask for clarification
  and acknowledge corrections.
- Respect privacy. Share only information you have permission to publish and
  remove credentials, personal details and household data from examples.
- Discuss hardware limitations and uncertain protocol interpretations honestly.
  Do not pressure others to perform unsafe tests.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption and publishing private information without consent are not
acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For content that violates GitHub's policies, use the **Report abuse to GitHub**
option or [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Abuse reports sent to GitHub go to GitHub, not directly to this project's
maintainers.

For project moderation, raise the concern in
[Discussions](https://github.com/DNier/proxon-hesp-homeassistant/discussions)
only if it can be described safely in public. Do not repost abusive material or
private information. There is no separate confidential maintainer contact for
conduct reports; use GitHub's reporting channel when privacy is needed.
The private vulnerability reporting channel is for security vulnerabilities,
as described in [SECURITY.md (German)](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, ask participants to change
their behaviour, or restrict participation. They should consider the context and
severity, explain decisions where appropriate without exposing private details,
and apply the same standards to themselves. Moderation is handled by volunteers;
an immediate response cannot be guaranteed.
